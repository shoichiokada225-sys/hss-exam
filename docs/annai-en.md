# For Everyone Taking the HSS Certification Exam

## Things to do before the day of the exam
1. Scan the QR code you were given with your smartphone camera
2. On the page that opens, select "Add to Home Screen" (this makes it an app)
3. Open the app once, and once you see the home screen, you may close it

※ If you skip this step, you may not be able to start the exam on the day if the signal is weak where you are.

## What to bring on the day
- Your own smartphone (charge it to 100%)
- Glasses (if you need them)

## Smartphone settings for the day
- Set date and time to "Automatic"
- Charge your phone fully

## Flow of the exam
1. At the venue, the exam supervisor will announce the password (it is not given in advance)
2. Enter your name exactly as it appears on your employee ID / roster
3. There are 60 questions and 60 minutes. You can go back to previous questions and change your answers
4. At the end, press "Finish the exam"
5. Do not close the app until you see "✅ Sent"

## Important notes
- If you leave the app during the exam (look at another app, close the screen), the number of times and the time are recorded and sent to the administrator
- If you have trouble (the screen goes blank, an error appears), don't panic — raise your hand and call the exam supervisor. You can resume partway through
- Results are not shown on the spot. You will be informed later by the company
- You can choose the language in the top right of the screen (Japanese / English / Tiếng Việt / Bahasa Indonesia / Español)

## For those who want to practice
You can practice with the app's "Demo Quiz" (5 questions, password 123, results are not sent anywhere)
